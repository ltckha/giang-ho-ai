# Little Shops of Dalat: English glossary and style rules

The game is "Gọn Phố". Its English title is **Little Shops of Dalat**. It is a cozy, mobile-first tower-defense game set in real streets of Đà Lạt, Vietnam. You are a shopkeeper. You place items on round pads to keep pests (rain drops, mud, sun rays, cockroaches, rats, mould, trash…) off your shelves. Every item and every pest belongs to one of the Five Elements.

## Style
- Warm, cozy, short, plain American English. UI labels must be as short as the Vietnamese or shorter. Buttons usually take 1–3 words.
- Keep every emoji, number and symbol (×, %, •, —, →, ★, ▲, ✔, ⚠️, 🔑) exactly as they are.
- Change decimal commas to points: "2,5" → "2.5".
- Placeholders `{0}`, `{1}`… must ALL appear in the English, each exactly once. You may reorder them. Never translate or remove them. A placeholder can stand for a number, a name or a whole phrase. Read the source file to see what it is.
- When a string is a fragment of a larger sentence (it starts or ends mid-sentence), translate it as a fragment that still reads naturally next to its neighbours. Look at the source file for context.
- Keep the same capitalisation style. Title Case in Vietnamese → Title Case in English. ALL CAPS stays ALL CAPS.
- Wordplay: aim for a fun, natural English name, not a literal word-by-word one.

## Names and places
- **Real places keep their Vietnamese spelling with all diacritics.** This covers real street and road names, wards, landmarks and the 34 province names. Swap the word order for "Phố/Đường X" → "X Street", "Phường X" → "X Ward", "Chợ X" → "X Market", "Hồ X" → "X Lake". For example, "Phố Tăng Bạt Hổ – Trương Công Định" → "Tăng Bạt Hổ – Trương Công Định Street". Write the city as **Dalat**.
- **Invented in-game names** (shops, streets, bosses, waves, items, pests, skills) get a fun English name. For example, "Tiệm Giày Hẻm Dốc" → "Slope Alley Shoe Shop", "Phố Dốc" → "Slope Street", "Trùm Bão Lốc" → "Boss Whirlwind".
- **Real or realistic business names:** keep the proper-name part and translate the shop type. For example, "Tiệm Bánh Quà Mimosa" → "Mimosa Gift Bakery", "Quán Phở Bằng" → "Phở Bằng".
- **Vietnamese foods keep their Vietnamese name**: phở, bánh mì, bánh căn, bánh tráng nướng, chè, cà phê phin, lẩu gà lá é, sữa đậu nành, nem nướng, bánh ướt lòng gà, yaourt… In a short label write only the Vietnamese food name. In a longer description, add a 2–4 word English gloss the first time, for example "bánh căn (mini rice pancakes)".
- Keep "homestay", "hostel" and "café" as normal English words.
